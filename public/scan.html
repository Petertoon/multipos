How to scan

  1. Tap the green Start camera button. Allow camera access if the phone asks.
  2. The button turns red while the camera is on.
  3. Hold the phone 4–8 inches from the barcode, with the bars running up and down inside the white box.
  4. Hold still for a second. A beep and a green flash mean it worked, and the item appears below and on the register.
  5. "Not found" means that barcode isn't in Products yet. Add it there, or tap "Enter a price for it".
  6. If it won't read: move a little farther back so it can focus, turn on the light, or tap Type barcode and type the numbers under the bars.
